File: `dock-promo-30s-en.mp4` · source: `index-en.html`

Same structure and timing as the Taiwan cut (`STORYBOARD.md`). All on-screen copy, the sample deck and the subtitles are in English, the budget is in USD, and the type is Inter / Space Grotesk / Archivo Black.
Tone: the first ~8 s is absurd and loud (acid lime × hot pink, stickers, flies, a soda can with googly eyes). From the Dock logo onward it switches to the clean, dark Dock UI. It ends on a white card with the slogan and logo.
English subtitles only, no voice-over; music and SFX are synthesized and timed to the cuts.

## Sample deck: DAILY FIZZ × Good Day Creative (fictional brand and agency)

1. Cover: “Make every Wednesday a mini holiday.” (2027 Integrated Campaign Pitch)
2. Insight: “73% of Gen Z say Monday to Friday is just waiting for the weekend”, plus a weekly mood index that bottoms out on Wednesday
3. Big idea: Wednesday Holiday. #WednesdayHoliday
4. Strategy:
   - Tease: #WednesdayOOO, where creators post their Wednesday out-of-office notes
   - Launch: Pop-up Wednesdays in six cities
   - Sustain: “How do you Wednesday?”, a UGC challenge plus retail collabs
5. Media mix: $600K, split Social 40% / Creators 25% / Out-of-home 20% / Retail 15%
6. KPIs: 120M impressions, 4.8% engagement, +18% favorability, +32% Wednesday sales, plus a monthly forecast chart
7. Key visual: “Take Wednesday off.” poster, an Instagram post, and an OOH line: “Wednesday off? It's on us.”
8. Thank you and next steps: Nov 15 sign-off → Dec 1 final KV → Jan 2027 launch

All figures are illustrative and are labelled that way on the slides.
